# Project Title

Heart Disease Prediction

## Problem Statement

Perform statistical analysis on patient clinical features (age, cholesterol, blood pressure, etc.) to assess associations with heart disease. The focus is on classical statistical methods rather than building machine-learning models. Planned analyses include:

-   Regression (linear and logistic, where appropriate)
-   Analysis of Variance (ANOVA)
-   Hypothesis testing and estimation of effect sizes
-   Model diagnostics, assumptions checking, and sensitivity analyses
-   Descriptive statistics and exploratory data analysis (visualization, summary tables)

All analyses should be reproducible, well-documented, and aimed at clear interpretation of risk factors and their statistical significance.
